How Villages Works

Getting started is simple. Build your trust network, find help in your community, and exchange value—all in three easy steps.

1

Endorse

Start by endorsing people you trust. Give them "hours"—a credit limit showing how much value you'd exchange with them. This builds the foundation of your trust network and lets them transact with people you don't even know yet.

2

Discover & Connect

Browse listings from your extended network. Find tutors, handymen, babysitters, or someone to share their garden harvest. Every person comes with a trust score based on their connection to you through the network.

3

Exchange

When someone helps you, acknowledge their contribution with Village Hours. These aren't debts—they're gentle promises to return the favor. The system routes payments through your trust network automatically.

The Magic of Ripple Payments

Your trust network makes it possible to exchange with people you've never met, through people you both trust.

Payments Flow Through Trust

Villages uses a technology called Ripple to route payments through your network. When you want to pay someone you don't directly know, the system finds a path of trust connecting you.

Each person on the path only exchanges promises with people they've personally endorsed. This means strangers can trade safely through mutual friends.

Example: How It Works

You want to pay Bob for tutoring, but you don't know Bob. However, your friend Emma has endorsed Bob. The system routes your payment: You → Emma → Bob. You owe Emma (who you trust), and Emma owes Bob (who she trusts). Everyone only deals with people they know!

Villages Hours: A Currency Based on Time

Our community currency is backed by something everyone has: time.

What is a Village Hour Worth?

One Village Hour represents approximately one hour of basic labor—a sustainable wage that varies by community but remains stable as a measure of value.

Unlike volatile currencies, Hours are grounded in real human effort. Skilled work can be valued at multiple hours per clock-hour, based on the expertise involved.
